Files in this folder (books, maps, app-store, photos, files, settings, notes, wallet, mail .webp)
are the light-theme icons from that file, exported as SVG on 2026-10-28 and changed by
scripts/ios18-icons.mjs: each icon's own rounded clip removed (the site's squircle mask is the
edge), rendered at 180 x 180 and saved as WebP. No other changes.
